Menu spelling: the art garbles two items ("NERULA TONC", "ASTRT RUM"). We use the
intended names, Nebula Tonic and Astro Rum. A small pink sign inside the bar is
unreadable in the art and is left as glow only.

## The signature: orb lamps

A wide round ring hangs over the bar, lit warm amber underneath, with ~8 glass
flask lamps hanging from it on chains. Each globe glows a different colour:
orange, amber, blue-violet, rose, magenta. Drinks are served in matching
round-bottomed glowing flasks.

## Bar, seats and lounge

- Bar counter of riveted dark steel, LED edge pink on the left half, cyan on the right.
- Back shelves of warm-lit bottles under the ring.
- Stools: one red-and-silver metal stool with four splayed legs and a crossbar,
  dented silver barrel stools (one with a crown mark), a blue metal stool. No names.
- Right side lounge: curved red leather booths along a big window, round
  glowing tables, crates and steel drums as side tables.
- Floor: dark wet tiles mirroring pink, cyan and amber.

## Look and light

Riveted dark steel with amber strip lights in the ceiling; pink/purple and cyan
neon; big window onto space with a ringed planet and disco-ball style orbs.
