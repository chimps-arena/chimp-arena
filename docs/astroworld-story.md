# Astroworld: the story (draft for the founders)

Everything here is built from what the team has already made: the NFT art,
Astro-News issue #2187, the posters in the backgrounds ("Smuggle. Rebel.
Repeat.", "Hunt. Track. Collect.", "Prison Dock 7", "A brighter tomorrow"),
and the ships, kits and properties. Names and facts can change; this is a
starting point to react to.

## The setting

**Year 2175.** Astroworld is a mining world on the edge of chimp space, and
the richest rock in the sector: its crust is threaded with **Void Crystal**,
the stuff that powers every ship, weapon and city in the galaxy.

**Astrocorp** built Astroworld from nothing. Its tower stands in the middle of
the **Core**, a walled city under the Astrocorp crown where nobody may draw a
weapon. Its slogan is on every wall: *A brighter tomorrow.* Astrocorp keeps
the peace, runs the Exchange, owns the docks and the starports, and wants
everyone to believe the boom will last forever.

Outside the wall, the rules stop. Three districts grew up along the roads:

- **The Belt**: claims, quarries and foundries. Where the ore comes out of
  the ground, and where most people get hurt.
- **Lunar Rim**: domes, greenhouses and observatories on the cold side. Food,
  water, science, and the people who'd rather be left alone.
- **Helios Docks**: hangars, fuel lots and docks. Everything that leaves
  Astroworld leaves through here, legally or not.

## The factions

- **Astrocorp** and **Astromilitary**: the order. Castro runs the patrols,
  the Astroguards hold the gates.
- **The Imperial Dynasty**: Napoleon, Cleopatra, Caesar and their court. Old
  money buying up the new world, one luxury property at a time.
- **The rebels**: smugglers and runners who think the crystal belongs to the
  people who dig it. *Smuggle. Rebel. Repeat.* Kill Boy Tommy is their legend.
- **The hunters**: hooded bounty hunters (Death, Dex, Deezy, Foxtrot,
  Kanuchi). They work for whoever pays. *Hunt. Track. Collect.*
- **The workers**: Dock 7, the foundries, the Prison Dock 7 crews in orange.
  The people actually holding Astroworld up.

## The hook

Kill Boy Tommy has vanished from Sector 7 with something Astrocorp badly
wants back: **a map of the richest Void Crystal seam on Astroworld** (decided
by the founders, 2026-11-22). Players don't learn what it is until Chapter 2;
in Chapter 1 it's only "something that belongs to Astrocorp". Astrocorp has put **500,000 $CHIMP** on his head (Astro-News, front
page). Every hunter in the sector is coming to Astroworld, the Dynasty is
buying up property while everyone's distracted, and the rebels are moving
crystal out through the Docks faster than ever.

You've just landed in the Core. Everyone wants something from you.

## Chapters

1. **Arrival** (in the game now). Get your bearings: meet Astromilitary,
   read the news, eat at Astroburger, visit a property, take a ship out to a
   district, and find the bounty hunter who's been asking about Tommy.
2. **The Sector 7 Manhunt** (next). Follow Tommy's trail through the three
   districts: clues at the Belt's claims, a contact in the Rim's domes, a
   smuggler at the Docks. The reveal: Tommy has the map of the richest
   crystal seam on Astroworld, the one Astrocorp never declared. Whoever holds
   it controls the boom. Choose who gets it: Astrocorp (order and the
   official reward), the hunters (a bigger cut) or the rebels (the crystal
   for the people who dig it). The choice decides your faction.
3. **The Games** (later). The Gladiator Arena opens (Astro-News, "The games
   commence"): ranked fights for status and prizes.
4. **The Grand Prix** (later). The rover racing circuit around the districts.
